# Engagement and operations

Participants opt into a daily reminder and Sunday evening reflection with `/remind HH:mm`, in their saved timezone. `/remind off` disables both; `/pause` stops them until `/resume`. Quiet hours default to 21:00–08:00 and can be changed with `/quiet HH:mm HH:mm`. Scheduled messages use each person's saved text/audio preference and appear in the chat where they opted in.

Schedule markers and outgoing entries commit atomically. A queued reminder is rechecked immediately before delivery: pause/off/leave/expiration cancel it and quiet hours defer it. Messages already accepted by Telegram cannot be recalled. Each scheduled item expires at the next local midnight; there is no burst of old missed reminders after an outage. The delivery queue rate gate limits sends conservatively.

Weekly challenges are optional, use one focus habit, and award one credit per completed local day regardless of the reported volume. Scores are completed days divided by elapsed days since joining that week's challenge. Keep the habit fixed for the week; leaving/rejoining does not reset the start date. Goals have different difficulty, so a score is a light social game, not a measure of ability or worth. Only active, opted-in sharing members appear, scoped to the requesting chat. Personal timezones determine each participant's week/day; a near-midnight comparison may span different local dates.

The authenticated internal endpoint `GET /api/operations/queues` returns queue status counts and oldest timestamps for operational monitoring. It contains no message bodies or keys. Use a host alert/monitor against queue age and failed counts; health checks alone do not prove Telegram or AI delivery works. Configure real alerting before production.

A daily retention task clears abandoned pending coaching input after one day, deletes terminal job/outbox content and inbound dedup IDs after seven days, retains content-free global usage aggregates for 30 days, and retains pseudonymous speech throttling counters for at most two UTC dates (cleanup runs daily at 03:00 UTC). Normal habits/check-ins remain until the participant requests deletion; `/forget confirm` removes their chat-specific records. Backups have a separate retention policy to configure before launch. Telegram messages and provider retention are separate from application deletion. A consent revocation/deletion cannot recall network requests that have already begun.
